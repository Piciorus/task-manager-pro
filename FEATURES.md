# Task Manager Pro - Complete Feature List

## 🎯 Unique Features Not Found in Most Task Managers

### 1. Energy Level Tracking ⚡
**What it is:** Assign an energy level (low/medium/high) to each task based on mental/physical effort required.

**Why it's unique:** Most task managers don't consider your energy levels throughout the day. This feature helps you:
- Schedule high-energy tasks when you're most alert
- Save low-energy tasks for when you're tired
- Optimize productivity by matching tasks to your energy

**How it works:**
- Set energy level when creating a task
- Get AI-powered suggestions based on time of day
- Morning (6-12): High energy tasks suggested
- Afternoon (12-18): Medium energy tasks
- Evening (18+): Low energy tasks

### 2. Smart AI Task Suggestions 🤖
**What it is:** Pattern-based task recommendations that learn from your habits.

**Why it's unique:** The system analyzes:
- Your current time of day
- Your typical productivity patterns
- Energy levels you've set for tasks
- Suggests the best tasks to work on right now

### 3. Productivity Score System 🏆
**What it is:** Real-time calculation of your productivity with gamification.

**Why it's unique:** Combines multiple metrics:
- Tasks completed (weighted by priority)
- Current streak days
- Completion patterns
- Generates a score out of 1000

### 4. Task Dependencies with Visual Mapping 🔗
**What it is:** Define which tasks depend on others completing first.

**Why it's unique:**
- See blocked tasks at a glance
- Automatic dependency resolution
- Visual dependency chains
- Prevents starting tasks too early

### 5. Voice Notes for Tasks 🎙️
**What it is:** Attach voice recordings to any task.

**Why it's unique:**
- Quick capture on mobile
- Future: Auto-transcription
- Perfect for meetings or commuting
- No need to type everything

### 6. Habit Integration 📝
**What it is:** Seamlessly integrate daily habits with your task workflow.

**Why it's unique:**
- Track habits alongside tasks
- Streak counting
- Frequency settings (daily/weekly/monthly)
- Completion calendar view

### 7. Peak Productivity Hours Analysis 📊
**What it is:** Automatic detection of when you're most productive.

**Why it's unique:**
- Analyzes completion patterns
- Identifies your best hours
- Helps schedule important tasks
- No manual tracking needed

### 8. Time Blocking with Conflict Detection ⏰
**What it is:** Visual time blocking calendar with smart scheduling.

**Why it's unique:**
- Drag-and-drop interface
- Automatic conflict warnings
- Color-coded by priority
- Integrates with task energy levels
